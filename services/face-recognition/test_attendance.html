Attendance Face Match — Test Page

Upload a stored profile photo, then use the webcam to capture a short burst. A blink is required during that burst — the guide circle turns green the moment a real blink is detected, and the check will be rejected server-side if no blink happened. This exercises POST /api/v1/attendance/mark-burst (recommended) or falls back to POST /api/v1/attendance/mark for plain photo uploads (no blink check possible on a single still photo).
